# Faisal Naseem website

A responsive, static one-page website for Faisal Naseem's Legal, Taxation & Management Consultancy practice.

## Open locally

Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder from any static web server.

## Before publishing

1. Add the firm's verified email address, telephone number, chambers address and LinkedIn profile to the contact and footer areas in `index.html`.
2. Connect the contact form in `script.js` to an approved form service, CRM, or backend endpoint. It is intentionally demonstration-only until a receiving destination is supplied.
3. Have the final public wording reviewed for compliance with applicable Bar Council of India requirements.
